About me

I'm a B.Tech Civil Engineering student at Sri Prakash College of Technology. I like turning 2D drawings into coordinated 3D models, so a building can be checked, discussed and improved before anyone starts construction.

My work so far covers building designs and the planning of a 4-lane national highway. I use AutoCAD for drafting, Revit for architectural and structural BIM models, and SketchUp for quick, clear 3D design views.

I'm looking for my first role as a BIM modeler or SketchUp designer, where I can keep learning on real projects.
